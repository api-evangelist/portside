# Portside

> Cloud-based business aviation management software (Cary, NC; backed by Insight Partners). The Portside Platform is a suite of modular applications for corporate flight departments, charter and managed fleets, aircraft management companies, business jet owners, and government/military aviation across 30+ countries. Portside does not publish a public developer API or self-service developer portal — integrations are arranged directly with the Portside team.
